
Badges are trainer abilities that give bonuses to their pokemon
Badges have team requirements such as 3+ Water Pokemon, All legendary etc

Gym Badges are given out by gym masters
Normal Badges can be found and given in all sorts of ways

- Prof Oak assistants give out badges for pokedex progress
- ➔ convert them to collectors club
- all HM rewards are converted to badges
- all dungeons reward bagdes
- Arcade rewards
- side quest rewards

**Vine Badge**  Binds deal 50% more damage and reduce the Def by one stage

**Fight Badge** - Your pokemon have Increased Critical Hit chance and accuracy

Combo Badge - Using a new move in a row adds bonus power. repeating a move in the chain resets the combo.

**Wind Badge** - Chance to gain favorable winds each turn ( +20% speed and bonus damage on wind attacks)

**Cryo Badge** - Frost moves have a 20% chance to decrease foe speed

Bug Badge - 20% chance to use String Shot at the end of each turn

MonoSpecies - pokemon gain power equal to the number of times its species has been switched in this battle
